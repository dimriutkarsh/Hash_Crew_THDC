    Why Document Validation Matters

    Helping citizens avoid rejections, delays, and endless back-and-forth.

    The Problem: 30% Rejection Rate

    Thousands of government applications are rejected every year simply because a document was blurry, missing, or in the wrong format. Citizens lose weeks waiting for a fix.

    The Solution: Pre-Submission AI

    Before the citizen clicks 'Submit', the system acts as a virtual clerk. It instantly checks for missing files, invalid formats, blurriness, and mismatched documents.

    The 6 Simulated AI Checks

    1. Missing Files

    Did you upload all 3 required docs?

    2. Format Check

    Is it PDF, JPG, or PNG?

    3. Size Check

    Is the file under 5 MB?

    4. Clarity Check

    Is the image blurry or dark?

    5. Type Matching

    Did you upload a bill instead of income proof?

    6. Completeness

    Are all pages included?
