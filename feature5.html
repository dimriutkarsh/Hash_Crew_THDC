    PROTOTYPE DEMO

    How AI Duplicate Detection Works

    Identifying multiple reports of the same issue without losing individual complaints.

    Step 1: Multiple Complaints

    Citizens Report an Issue

    Different citizens in the same area (e.g., "Example Village") submit complaints. They might describe the same road damage using different words.

    Step 2: AI Analysis

    Similarity Matching

    The AI compares the complaints. It looks at:

    • Matching Category (e.g., Road Damage)
    • Matching Location (e.g., Example Village)
    • Common Keywords in descriptions (e.g., "potholes", "school road")
    Step 3: Group Creation

    Simulated Similarity Score

    If the score is above 70%, the system groups them into a single "Issue Group" (e.g., GRP-001). Important: Individual complaints are not deleted or merged.

    3 complaints linked to one common road issue.
    Step 4: Official Action

    Monitoring & Efficiency

    Officials see one grouped issue affecting 3 citizens, rather than three separate tickets. They can prioritize this area and update all citizens at once.
