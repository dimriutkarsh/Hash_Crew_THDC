Prayas 2026 Prototype

Feature 2: WhatsApp-Based Chatbot

Apuni Sarkar Smart Assist – Government services via WhatsApp

PROTOTYPE DEMO

Citizen opens WhatsApp

The user sends a message like "मुझे आय प्रमाण पत्र बनवाना है।" (I need an income certificate).

AI understands the request

The chatbot replies in Hindi and identifies the service: Income Certificate. It lists required documents (Aadhaar, income proof, residence proof) and the process (online application).

Service guidance

A clickable button Apply for Income Certificate appears. When clicked, the bot explains the steps and asks: "Do you want to continue?"

Application assistance

After "Yes, Continue", a simple form appears (Name, Mobile, Documents). Submit → confirmation: "Your application has been prepared successfully."

Prototype only. This is a demonstration for Prayas 2026 — no applications are actually submitted to any government portal. The video above shows the complete WhatsApp interaction.
