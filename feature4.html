    How the Verification System Works

    Ensuring complaints aren't closed until the citizen confirms resolution.

    Step 1: Registration

    Citizen Files a Complaint

    The citizen submits details and an image. The system generates a unique Complaint ID and a secret Verification Code (only the citizen knows this code).

    Step 2: Official Action

    Department Resolves the Issue

    The assigned official views the complaint, updates the status to "In Progress", fixes the issue, uploads Resolution Evidence (Photo), and submits it for verification.

    Step 3: Citizen Verification

    The Final Check

    The citizen receives a notification. They review the official's proof. Officials cannot close the complaint. The citizen must enter their secret Verification Code to close it.

    Resolved: Enter Code & Close Not Resolved: Reopen
    Step 4: Deadline & Escalation

    Accountability Mechanism

    Every complaint has a 10-day deadline. If the official fails to submit a resolution in time, the system automatically flags it as Overdue and escalates it to a Senior Officer.
