Feature 3: WhatsApp-Based Chatbot

Apuni Sarkar Smart Assist – Government services via WhatsApp

PROTOTYPE DEMO

How the WhatsApp Bot Works

A step-by-step flow of the citizen's experience.

Step 1: User Opens WhatsApp

Citizen sends a message

The user simply messages the bot in Hindi: "मुझे आय प्रमाण पत्र बनवाना है।" (I want to make an income certificate).

Step 2: AI Understands

Bot identifies the service

The AI replies: "नमस्ते! मैं Apuni Sarkar Smart Assist हूँ..." and displays:

Service: Income Certificate

Required Documents: Aadhaar Card, Income Proof, Residence Proof

Process: Online application

Step 3: Service Guidance

Interactive Action Button

A clickable button appears: Apply for Income Certificate

When clicked, the bot explains the steps and asks the citizen: "Do you want to continue?"

Step 4: Application

Form & Confirmation

After clicking "Yes, Continue", a simple form appears in the chat asking for Name, Mobile Number, and Documents.

Upon submission: "Your application has been prepared successfully."

Prototype only. This is a demonstration for Prayas 2026 — no applications are actually submitted to any government portal. The video above shows the complete WhatsApp interaction.
