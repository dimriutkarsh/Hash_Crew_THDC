    About Apuni Sarkar

    Apuni Sarkar is an e-governance initiative by the Government of Assam bringing citizen services under one digital roof. It aims to reduce visits to government offices and make services accessible from any device.

    This prototype, built for Prayas 2026, demonstrates the service catalogue, a guided application flow, and an AI assistant that opens the correct form based on a citizen's request.
