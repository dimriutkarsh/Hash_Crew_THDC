    Government Services, No Internet Required

    Bridging the digital divide for citizens in remote areas.

    The Problem: The Digital Barrier

    Millions of citizens in Uttarakhand lack smartphones, stable internet, or the digital literacy to navigate complex government portals. They rely on expensive cyber cafes or middlemen, leading to delays and fraud.

    The Solution: A Phone Call

    By dialing a toll-free number, citizens can speak to an AI in their local language. The AI acts as a virtual clerk—guiding them, explaining required documents, and registering complaints instantly.
