    The Journey of a Single Click

    How Devbhoomi ID integration transforms a 20-minute form into a 2-minute confirmation.

    1. The Consent-First Verification

    What happens: The citizen enters their Family ID and checks a consent box.

    Why it matters: Data privacy is paramount. The system does not fetch data silently. By requiring explicit consent, we ensure the citizen is in total control of their family's demographic data.

    2. The Family Snapshot

    What happens: The system retrieves a list of all family members linked to the ID (e.g., Rahul, Sunita, Aman).

    Why it matters: A single parent can easily apply for a scholarship for their child, or a certificate for their spouse, without needing to re-enter their names, ages, or relationships manually.

    3. The Auto-Prefill Magic

    What happens: When applying for an Income Certificate, the applicant's name, district, village, and relationship to the head are instantly filled in.

    Why it matters: It eliminates typographical errors (e.g., misspelling a village name) that cause government applications to be rejected. The data is guaranteed to match state records.

    4. Final Review & Local Save

    What happens: The citizen reviews the auto-filled data, adds any missing mobile numbers or specific details, and confirms.

    Why it matters: The citizen remains the final authority. The application is then saved locally, ready to be pushed to the government server when the backend is integrated.
